## About the Folvio integration

Writing a postmortem starts with collecting what happened. Navy does that part for you: when an incident is resolved, Folvio gets a page with the full timeline, every alert, response times and who acknowledged what, ready for the team to add the why.

## Key features

- **Timeline included:** Every alert, update and recovery in order.
- **Your template:** Use your own postmortem structure.
- **Responders mentioned:** Everyone who worked the incident is tagged on the page.
- **Linked back:** The incident in Navy links to the finished postmortem.

## Get started

In Navy, open Settings, then Integrations, and choose Folvio. Connect a space, pick a template and decide which severities get a postmortem.

For help, read the [Folvio setup guide](#_).
