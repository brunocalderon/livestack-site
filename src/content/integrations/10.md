## About the Quillet integration

Most customers will never visit your status page, but they do visit your site. The Quillet integration adds a status badge to your footer and a banner during incidents, both updated live from Navy.

## Key features

- **Status badge:** A small footer badge that links to your status page.
- **Incident banner:** Shown only while an incident is open.
- **Your styles:** Inherits the fonts and colours of your Quillet site.
- **No code:** Add the block from the Quillet editor.

## Get started

In Quillet, open the block library, add the Navy status block and sign in. Pick the status page you want to show.

For help, read the [Quillet setup guide](#_).
