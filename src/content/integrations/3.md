## About the Ostrel integration

Support usually hears about an outage from customers. With Ostrel connected, the incident lands in the support inbox as soon as Navy confirms it, with a ready answer the team can send while engineers work on the fix.

## Key features

- **One conversation per incident:** Every update is added as an internal note, not a new email.
- **Auto tagging:** Tag conversations with the affected component.
- **Saved replies:** Insert the current incident summary into any reply.
- **Auto close:** Close the incident conversation when the monitor is back up.

## Get started

In Navy, open Settings, then Alerts, and choose Ostrel. Connect your account, pick an inbox and decide which monitors should open a conversation.
