## About the Lumcode integration

The Lumcode extension brings Navy into your editor. Hover over a route to see its uptime and response time, run a check from the command palette, and acknowledge an alert without leaving the file you are fixing.

## Key features

- **Inline status:** Uptime and latency next to the routes Navy monitors.
- **Run a check:** Test an endpoint from any region with one command.
- **Incident panel:** Follow open incidents in the sidebar.
- **Acknowledge in place:** Stop the escalation from the notification.

## Get started

Install the Navy extension from the Lumcode marketplace, sign in with your Navy account and choose a workspace.

For help, read the [Lumcode setup guide](#_).
