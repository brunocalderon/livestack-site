## About the Pellow integration

Pellow turns a Navy alert into a page on the phone of whoever is on call. Critical monitors escalate from a push notification to a call, and the page stops the moment someone acknowledges it in Pellow or in Navy.

## Key features

- **Schedule sync:** Navy reads your Pellow rotations, so the right person is paged without a second schedule.
- **Loud for critical:** Choose which monitors can call and which only send a push.
- **Two-way acknowledge:** Acknowledge in either app and the other one updates.
- **Quiet hours:** Hold low priority alerts until the morning.

## Get started

In Navy, open Settings, then Alerts, and choose Pellow. Sign in, map your Navy escalation policies to Pellow rotations and send a test page.
