## About the Brevan integration

Customers look for news about an outage wherever they already are. The Brevan integration posts each status page update on your account and threads the follow ups under the first post, so the whole story reads in order.

## Key features

- **Threaded updates:** Every update is posted as a reply to the first one.
- **Choose the severity:** Only share major incidents if you prefer.
- **Edit before posting:** Review the text before it goes out, or post automatically.
- **Resolved post:** Close the thread with the total downtime.

## Get started

In Navy, open your status page settings and choose Brevan. Connect the account you want to post from and choose which updates to share.
