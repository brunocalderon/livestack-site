## About the Drafta integration

Your status page should look like the rest of your product. Design it in Drafta with the Navy kit, then sync it: colours, type, logo and favicon move to your Navy status page without anyone copying values by hand.

## Key features

- **Navy kit:** Every status page component, ready to style.
- **One click sync:** Push colours, fonts and images to Navy.
- **Light and dark:** Sync both themes from one file.
- **Preview:** See the live status page before you publish.

## Get started

Duplicate the Navy kit in Drafta, style it, then open your status page settings in Navy, choose Drafta and pick the file to sync.

For help, read the [Drafta setup guide](#_).
