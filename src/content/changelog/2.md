Status pages can now keep your customers informed without anyone on your team sending a message. Visitors can **subscribe by email** and get every incident update and maintenance notice as it is posted. And your status page can now live on **your own domain**, on a status subdomain of the site your customers already know.

**Key Highlights:**

- **Email subscribers:** Customers subscribe from the status page and get incident updates, maintenance reminders and resolution notices.
- **Component subscriptions:** Subscribers can choose only the components they care about.
- **Custom domains:** Point a CNAME to Navy and we issue and renew the SSL certificate for you.
- **Private pages:** Restrict a status page to your team or to signed-in customers.

This release turns your status page into the first place customers hear from you during an incident. Subscribers and custom domains are available on every plan.
