Navy works best when everyone who might be on call has an account. Invited members can acknowledge alerts, update incidents and post to your status page.

## Send invitations

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Click **Invite** and enter one or more email addresses.
3. Choose a role for the group and click **Send**.

Invitations expire after seven days. You can resend them from the same page.

## Roles

- **Owner:** Full access, including billing and deleting the workspace.
- **Admin:** Manage monitors, alert policies, members and status pages.
- **Responder:** Acknowledge alerts, update incidents and edit monitors.
- **Viewer:** Read-only access to monitors, incidents and reports.

## Single sign-on

On the Business plan, members can sign in with Okta, Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra ID. Open **Settings**, then **Security**, and follow the steps for your provider. Once SSO is on, you can require it for every member.
