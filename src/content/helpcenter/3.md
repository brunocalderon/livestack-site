An escalation policy is a list of steps. Each step notifies one person, a schedule or a channel, and waits for someone to acknowledge the alert before moving on.

## Create the policy

1. Open **On-call**, then **Escalation policies**.
2. Click **New policy** and give it a name, such as "API, business hours".
3. Add the first step. Most teams start with the current person on an on-call schedule.

## Add more steps

For each step, set how long Navy should wait before escalating. Five minutes is a good default for the first step. A typical policy looks like this:

1. Page the on-call engineer by push notification and SMS.
2. After 5 minutes, call the on-call engineer.
3. After 10 more minutes, page the secondary schedule.
4. After 15 more minutes, notify the engineering lead.

## Attach it to monitors

Open a monitor, go to **Alerts** and choose the policy. You can also attach a policy to a tag, so every monitor with that tag uses it automatically.

## Repeat and resolve

Turn on **Repeat** to run the policy again if nobody acknowledges it after the last step. When the monitor recovers, everyone who was notified gets a recovery message.
