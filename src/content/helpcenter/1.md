A monitor is a check that Navy runs on a schedule from several regions. When a check fails in three regions, Navy opens an incident and starts your alert policy.

## Add the monitor

1. In the dashboard, click **New monitor**.
2. Choose a type. **HTTP** covers most websites and APIs. Use **Keyword** to also check that the response contains a word or phrase.
3. Paste the URL you want to check.
4. Pick a check interval. Free plans check every 3 minutes, paid plans every 30 seconds.

## Choose regions

By default, every monitor runs from all 18 regions. If your service is only available in some parts of the world, open **Regions** and select the ones that should run the check. Keep at least three, since an outage is confirmed only when three regions agree.

## Set up alerts

Under **Alerts**, choose who gets notified. You can start with your own email and add Slack, SMS or an escalation policy later.

## Save and verify

Click **Create monitor**. The first checks run within a minute and appear on the monitor page with response times from each region. To see an alert end to end, click **Send test alert**.
