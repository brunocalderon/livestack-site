Custom domains are available on the Team and Business plans. Navy issues and renews the certificate for you.

## Add the domain

1. Open your status page and go to **Settings**, then **Domain**.
2. Enter the domain you want to use, for example status.yourcompany.test.
3. Navy shows the DNS record you need to add.

## Update your DNS

In your DNS provider, create a **CNAME** record that points your status subdomain to the target shown in Navy. Changes usually take a few minutes, but can take up to an hour.

## Verify

Back in Navy, click **Verify**. Once the record is found, Navy issues a certificate and the domain shows as active. Your old navy.test address keeps working and redirects to the new domain.

## Troubleshooting

- **Verification fails:** Make sure there is no other record, such as an A record, on the same name.
- **Certificate pending:** If you use a proxy in front of your domain, turn it off until the certificate is issued.
